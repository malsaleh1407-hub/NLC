How Arrangement Works

Drag-and-drop to reorder categories (the order they appear on products.html) and products within each category.

  1. Drag items using the handle on the left.
  2. Click Save Order, the browser stores your arrangement locally and downloads products-order.json.
  3. Upload products-order.json to the site root (same folder as products.html) on nlc.com.sa.
  4. The live site reads this file on load and applies your order.

Note: The "Preview" button applies your order in your browser without saving, useful for testing before exporting.
