Sony A7R IV editorial look, shallow depth of field, teal-and-orange cinematic grade, photorealistic. Light behaviour is the hero of the shot.

**PRODUCT LOCK**
> The ceiling luminaires are exactly the ALPHA recessed COB downlight shown in the reference images: round and flush-mounted, a wide smooth matte-white circular bezel, a deep faceted silver aluminium reflector cone, and one small round warm COB LED at the centre. **NEGATIVE**
> No text, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no signage, no brand names, no purple or pink neon, no cold blue-only grade, no cartoon or CGI look, no lens flare overload, no distorted geometry.
