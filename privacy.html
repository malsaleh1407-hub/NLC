Privacy Policy

How NLC – National Lighting Company collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

National Lighting Company ("NLC", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you visit nlc.com.sa, request a quote, or otherwise interact with us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly, name, company, email address, phone number, and the message or inquiry content you submit through our contact and newsletter forms.
  • Information collected automatically, IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and timestamps, gathered via cookies and similar technologies to operate the website and measure usage.
  • Information from third parties, occasionally, information from business partners, referral sources, and publicly available business directories for B2B engagement purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries, prepare quotations, and fulfill orders.
  • To send service updates, product information, and, where you have opted in, marketing communications.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases: your consent, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate business interests.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who support our website and business operations (email delivery, analytics, cloud hosting), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorized business partners where required to provide a requested service.
  • Government authorities when required by law.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies for basic functionality and optional analytics cookies to help us improve the site. You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable laws.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@nlc.com.sa.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside Saudi Arabia where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate protections are in place.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
