_Source: [`academy/ACADEMY_PROMPTS.md`](academy/ACADEMY_PROMPTS.md)_

Every visual asset for the 30 new academy episodes. Each episode follows the same 8-frame narrative arc the Lux vs Lumen pilot uses ([storyboards/lux-vs-lumen/PROMPTS.md](storyboards/lux-vs-lumen/PROMPTS.md)).

**Tools:**
- **Stills** → Google Gemini Nano Banana 2 (`gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview`). Web: [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com), 16:9, 2K. CLI: `~/.claude/skills/banana/scripts/generate.py`.
- **Motion** → Google Veo 3.1 Lite (image-to-video). 8 s, 1080p, 16:9, no audio. Each motion prompt is paired with the matching still from step 1.

**Save path convention:** stills go to `academy/storyboards/<slug>/01-hook.jpg` … `08-cta.jpg`. Motion clips go to `academy/video/<slug>/clips/01-hook.mp4` … `08-cta.mp4`.

## Master brand prefix — prepend to every still prompt

> Cinematic photographic, shot on Sony A7R IV with 24mm prime at f/5.6, dramatic teal-and-orange color grade — cool navy shadows (#24285e) and warm orange highlights (#F6851F), National Geographic industrial-tech editorial aesthetic. Saudi commercial/industrial setting — polished concrete or epoxy floors, deep navy painted walls, premium finishes (NEOM-style architecture). No readable text on any surface, no logos, no signage anywhere. Saturated navy and orange ONLY — desaturate any green or red. 16:9 aspect ratio.

## Master motion direction — append to every Veo prompt

> Keep camera moves subtle and slow — editorial energy, not music-video energy. Shutter feel of 1/50. No camera shake. No glitches. The brand palette (navy + orange) must hold across the full clip.

## The 8-frame narrative arc (same for every episode)

| # | Frame | Purpose | Typical composition |
|---|-------|---------|---------------------|
| 01 | **Hook** | Pose the question | Macro / object close-up |
| 02 | **Concept A** | Define the first idea | Single hero subject, clean |
| 03 | **Concept B** | Define the contrasting idea | Same subject, different state |
| 04 | **Relationship** | How A and B relate | Triptych or split / diagrammatic |
| 05 | **Applications** | Real Saudi setting | Wide editorial interior or exterior |
| 06 | **Mistake** | What goes wrong | Split before/after or evident failure |
| 07 | **NLC spec** | Engineer-led right answer | Saudi engineer in profile, working |
| 08 | **CTA** | Clean end card | Architectural / abstract negative space |

## Common Higgsfield gotchas

- **Faces drift across clips** — same engineer in episode 02 and episode 17 will look different. Either: use a single reference image consistently across image-to-video calls; or accept variation and crop tighter to reduce face visibility.
- **Text in scene is gibberish** — Higgsfield (and all AI video models in 2026) can't render real text. Don't try to bake in a streetlight nameplate or a control panel display. Overlay text in CapCut where you have pixel control.
- **Long takes break down** — Higgsfield holds quality for ~5-7 seconds. Generate two clips and cut between them rather than asking for one 12-second take. The brand grade keeps the cut invisible.
- **SF6 / arc / flicker effects** — describe as static visual cues; don't expect Higgsfield to animate fast physical effects realistically. Use explicit motion direction.
- **Saudi-specific people** — always include "Saudi engineer, navy thobe, white ghutra, trimmed black beard, 40s" or "Saudi technician, navy NLC polo, 30s" — Higgsfield default-stereotypes are Western.
