NLC Launches Smart Lighting Platform for Vision 2030 Projects

Our latest IoT-enabled lighting management system has been selected for major Vision 2030 infrastructure projects across the Kingdom. The platform delivers real-time monitoring, energy analytics, and predictive maintenance, reducing operational costs by up to 40% for municipalities and developers.

Integrating with existing infrastructure, the system supports DALI, Zigbee, and NB-IoT protocols, making NLC the premier smart lighting partner for Saudi Arabia's next chapter of development.
