Who We Are

National Lighting Company (NLC) is a leader in providing lighting, low-current systems, and power solutions across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and beyond.

Established with a vision to illuminate the future, NLC specializes in innovative lighting products and integrated solutions. With the strategic acquisitions of MDI and BMC in 2024, NLC has expanded to become a one-stop solution provider for lighting, low-current systems, and low-voltage power distribution.

Our Journey

1993

Founded in Dammam

  • NLC was incorporated in Dammam with the mission to deliver exceptional lighting solutions. The company emphasized innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction from the outset.
1998

Strategic Partnership & National Expansion

  • Formed a strategic partnership with LUXO Norway to exchange technology and knowledge.
  • Opened new offices in Riyadh and Jeddah, expanding the business footprint.
2004

Factory 1 Expansion

  • Expanded Factory 1 with Phase 1 development.
2010

Factory 1 Phase 2

  • Completed Phase 2 expansion of Factory 1.
2012

Factory 2 Construction

  • Initiated construction of Factory 2.
2014

Headquarters & Factory 2 Online

  • Completed the new headquarters building, covering 740 SQM.
  • Commenced operations at Factory 2.
2021

GCC Service Network

  • Expanded service network with new offices and partnerships across the GCC, enhancing customer support and service.
2024

Restructuring & Comprehensive Solutions

  • Overall restructuring led by Chairman Eng. Mubarak Alkhafrah, hiring professional experienced management to lead the operations.
  • Ali Alfayez joined as CEO in February 2024.
  • Acquired MDI and BMC to strengthen expertise in Low Current and LV switchgear, deepening market presence and shifting to a comprehensive solution offering instead of product offering.
  • Formed the NLC R&D team to localize the engineering behind lighting technology and product development.
2025

100% National & Localization

  • NLC transformed to a 100% national company as of 1 May 2025.
  • Grew from 120 employees in Feb 2024 to 210 employees by Feb 2025.
  • Renovated Factory 2 to improve lighting localization, opened the R&D Center, and localized the LED SMT process.
  • Digital Transformation & AI incorporation project ongoing - to be completed within 2025.

Chairman's Message

"For over 33 years, NLC has been at the forefront of illuminating Saudi Arabia's most iconic projects and public spaces. Our journey began in Dammam in 1993, and today we stand as a fully Saudi-owned company with a vision aligned with Vision 2030."

We have grown from a lighting specialist into a solutions provider, adding low-current systems and power & control through our acquisitions of MDI and BMC. The shift lets us own more of what we deliver to each client.

Our investment in local manufacturing, R&D, and talent reflects our belief in Saudi Arabia's industrial future. We are 100% Saudi-owned and committed to growing the Kingdom's industrial base.
