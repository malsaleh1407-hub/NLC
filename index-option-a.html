Who We Are

About NLC

National Lighting Company (NLC) is a leader in providing lighting, low-current systems, and power solutions across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and beyond.

Established with a vision to illuminate the future, NLC specializes in innovative lighting products and integrated solutions. With strategic acquisitions of MDI (2024) and BMC (2024), NLC has expanded to become a one-stop solution provider for lighting, low-current systems, and low-voltage power distribution.

Our Journey

1993

Founded in Dammam

National Lighting Company established in the Second Industrial City, Dammam, as Saudi Arabia's first dedicated LED luminaire manufacturer.

1998

Factory Expansion

Expanded production capacity with a second factory, covering over 20,000 square meters of modern manufacturing space.

2005

ISO Certification

Achieved ISO 9001 quality management certification, reinforcing our commitment to international manufacturing standards.

2010

First Saudi LED Manufacturer

Made history as the first Saudi company to manufacture LED luminaires domestically, pioneering the Kingdom's lighting revolution.

2015

National Expansion

Opened regional offices in Riyadh and Jeddah, extending NLC's reach across all major cities in the Kingdom.

2018

International Certifications

Earned UL (American), SASO (Saudi), and IEC (European) product certifications across the full product range.

2021

ESCO Approval

Received official ESCO (Energy Service Company) approval, enabling NLC to deliver energy efficiency solutions.

2024

BMC Acquisition & NEOM

Acquired BMC for Power & Control Systems division, and supplied lighting for the landmark NEOM Residential Communities project.

Leadership Message

Chairman's Message

"For over 33 years, NLC has been at the forefront of illuminating Saudi Arabia's most iconic projects and public spaces. Our journey began in Dammam in 1993, and today we stand as a fully Saudi-owned company with a vision aligned with Vision 2030."

We have grown from a lighting specialist into a solutions provider, encompassing low-current systems and power & control through our strategic acquisitions of MDI and BMC. This evolution reflects our commitment to delivering end-to-end value to our clients.

Our investment in local manufacturing, R&D, and talent development is a testament to our belief in Saudi Arabia's industrial future. We are proud to be 100% Saudi-owned and deeply committed to the Kingdom's economic transformation.
