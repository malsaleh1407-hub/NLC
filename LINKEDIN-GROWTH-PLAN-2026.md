## 4. Engine 1 — Employee advocacy (largest free lever)

NLC lists 1,001–5,000 employees. Assume ~400 have active LinkedIn profiles and ~250 can be realistically activated. Average KSA engineer/professional has 400–900 connections, heavily concentrated in exactly the target industries.

### Target: +1,800 followers

Modelled as: 250 activated employees × 3 follower conversions each over the period (≈750), plus the reach multiplier on page posts converting cold viewers (≈1,050).

### Mechanics

**Step 1 — Claim the employees.** Every employee whose LinkedIn "Company" field is free text or misspelled does not link to the page. Run an audit; ask HR to circulate the exact page URL so profiles resolve to the official page. This alone unlocks the "Notify employees" button and the employee-reach multiplier.

**Step 2 — Use "Notify employees of post."** Page admins can notify employees of a post **once every 24 hours**. Use it on the strongest post of each day (not all five). Employees get a notification; even a 15% click-through from 400 employees is 60 guaranteed early engagements, which is what the algorithm reads as a quality signal in the first 60 minutes.

**Step 3 — Tiered advocacy program.**

| Tier | Who | Ask | How to get compliance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Leadership (~15)** | GM, division heads, sales director, R&D head, factory manager | 1 personal post/week + reshare 2 page posts/week | Direct sponsor mandate; ghostwritten drafts supplied |
| **Tier 2 — Commercial + Engineering (~80)** | Sales, estimation, projects, R&D, QA | Reshare 2 page posts/week with 1 line of personal comment | Weekly WhatsApp broadcast with the link + a suggested comment |
| **Tier 3 — All staff (~250+)** | Everyone with a profile | React/comment on 2 posts/week | Monthly internal leaderboard; small recognition prize |

**Step 4 — Make it frictionless.** Every Sunday morning, a single WhatsApp/Teams broadcast goes out containing: the week's 5 post links, a copy-paste caption for each in EN and AR, and one instruction — *"Reshare with your own first line. Do not copy the same comment."* Identical copy-pasted comments get suppressed by LinkedIn; varied ones do not.

**Step 5 — Thought Leader Ads.** Sponsor the best-performing employee post each month (leadership posts convert far better than page posts). Budget included in §9.

### Guardrails
- Never make advocacy mandatory in writing — it reads as astroturf and staff resent it. Make it visible, rewarded and easy.
- No engagement pods, no "comment 🔥 below" schemes. LinkedIn demotes them and the audience is engineers who notice.

### 5.4 Posting cadence — Saudi work week

**5 posts/week, Sunday–Thursday.** Do not post Friday–Saturday.

| Day | Slot (AST) | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **Sunday** | 08:30 | Academy carousel (week's flagship) |
| **Monday** | 09:00 | Project proof / case study |
| **Tuesday** | 08:30 | Academy video (native, vertical) |
| **Wednesday** | 09:30 | People / factory / behind-the-scenes |
| **Thursday** | 08:00 | Standards take, poll, or news |

Best windows in KSA are **07:30–09:30** and **13:00–14:00 AST**. Sunday and Monday carry the strongest B2B attention; Thursday afternoon is dead.

### 5.5 Language policy

Bilingual is a growth advantage in this market, not overhead — but mixing badly halves reach.

- **Caption:** EN first (first ~200 characters must stand alone before "…see more"), then a divider, then the AR translation. Consultants and multinationals read EN; contractors, technicians and government read AR.
- **Creative:** produce **separate** EN and AR carousels. Do not cram both languages onto one slide.
- **Weekly split:** 3 EN-lead + 2 AR-lead posts. Post the AR version of the flagship carousel on **Tuesday**, three days after the EN version, to double the flagship's reach without cannibalising it.
- Arabic creative uses **Cairo** at RTL, per the site's own type system.

### 5.6 Post construction rules

1. **First line is 90% of the job.** It is the only thing most people read. Lead with a number, a cost, or a specific failure: *"A cable sized at 30 °C will overheat in a 50 °C Saudi MEP riser. Here's the derating factor most BOQs miss."*
2. **No links in the post body.** Put the link in the first comment or in the post's edit after 30 minutes. External links cut reach materially.
3. **3–5 hashtags maximum**, at the end. Standing set: `#SaudiArabia #Vision2030 #MEP #LightingDesign #MadeInSaudi` plus one topical.
4. **Tag deliberately, not desperately.** Tag partner companies (ABB), client organisations only when the post genuinely features them and permission exists, and the employees who appear in it. Never tag people who are not in the content.
5. **Respond to every comment within 2 hours** during the first 4 hours after posting. Comment replies are the strongest reach signal available and are entirely free.
6. **No stock photos of generic offices.** NLC has 3,200+ real projects and two factories. Use them.
7. **One idea per post.** Save the rest — there are 19 weeks to fill.

## 6. Engine 3 — Page invite credits

### Target: +650 followers

LinkedIn gives each company page a pool of **250 invitation credits per calendar month**, and **a credit is refunded when the invite is accepted**. Invites are sent to an admin's own 1st-degree connections.

**Mechanics:**
1. Appoint **10–15 page admins** across sales, projects, engineering and leadership. Credits are a shared page pool, but each admin can only invite *their own* connections — so more admins means a far larger and better-qualified eligible pool drawn from the same 250 credits.
2. Spend the full 250 credits in the first week of every month. Unspent credits do not roll over — leaving them unused is pure waste, and this happens on almost every B2B page.
3. Invite **selectively**, not in bulk. Filter to the ICP in §2. A 40%+ acceptance rate keeps refunding credits, so a disciplined 250 can turn into 350–450 sent invites in a month; a sloppy 250 with 10% acceptance dries up immediately.
4. Aug (partial) + Sep + Oct + Nov + Dec ≈ **1,600–1,900 invites sent at ~35–40% acceptance ≈ 600–700 followers.**

**Never** invite indiscriminately to hit volume — low acceptance destroys the refund loop, and it seeds the page with people who never engage, which drags reach on every future post.

## 7. Engine 4 — Newsletter + Events

### Target: +700 followers

### 7.1 LinkedIn Newsletter — "NLC Academy Weekly"

The most underused follower mechanism on LinkedIn: **subscribing to a page newsletter follows the page**, every new edition sends a notification *and* an email to all subscribers, and the launch of a newsletter prompts LinkedIn to invite the page's existing followers to subscribe.

- **Launch:** Week 2 (w/c 24 Aug). Launching once is a one-time reach event — do not waste it before the content system is running.
- **Cadence:** weekly, every Sunday, one Academy episode expanded to a 600–900 word article, EN with an AR summary block.
- **Effect:** each edition notifies every subscriber, which produces engagement the page would otherwise have to earn. Expect subscriber base ≈ 25–35% of followers.

### 7.2 LinkedIn Events / Live sessions

Run **4 events** (one per month, Sep–Dec), 40 minutes, Arabic and English:

| Month | Session | Hook for the target audience |
|---|---|---|
| Sep | Emergency Lighting & KSA Civil Defense compliance | "What inspectors actually check" |
| Oct | SASO / SABER for electrical products — clearing customs first time | Procurement pain |
| Nov | IEC 61439 type-tested assemblies — what "design-verified" doesn't cover | Contrarian, technical, tender-relevant |
| Dec | Lighting a Vision 2030 giga-project: lessons from NEOM & Diriyah | Aspirational, NLC-only credibility |

**Why this works for follower count:** every registrant can be invited to follow the page, event pages surface in feeds of registrants' networks, and a registration is a far stronger intent signal than a follow. Target 250–400 registrants per event.

## 13. Risks and what to do about them

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employee advocacy never activates** | High | Severe — this is the largest engine | Sponsor mandate from management in Week 1, not Week 6. If Tier 1 compliance is under 50% by 20 Sep, escalate to the GM and add SAR 15,000 to paid. |
| **Content production can't sustain 5 posts/week** | High | Severe | Build a 4-week buffer in P0 before launching. If the buffer drops under 2 weeks, cut to 4 posts/week rather than shipping weak posts — reach penalties from bad posts persist. |
| **Cost per follower runs above SAR 15** | Medium | Moderate | Tighten to company-list targeting only; shift budget from Campaign A to B (proven organic creative always converts cheaper). |
